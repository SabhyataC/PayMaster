Audit integrity

Every decision and payment is written to a log that cannot be edited or deleted, and each entry is cryptographically linked to the one before it.

Checking the chain…

Recomputing every link.

Head hash

A fingerprint of the whole log up to now. Keep a copy somewhere outside the database; if it ever differs for the same position, the log was rewritten.

–

How it works

Database rules refuse any change or deletion of an audit row. A background process chains the rows with SHA-256 within seconds of being written. Verifying recomputes the full chain and also checks that nothing was removed from its end.
